# End of a hand

A hand ends when someone wins or the hand ends in a draw.

This section covers these scenarios in more detail and introduces new terms like **honba** and **renchan**.

## Honba

**Honba** is a counter that adds bonus points to a winning hand.
- The **honba** counter **increases by one** if the dealer wins, or the hand ends in a draw.
- The **honba** counter **resets** if a non-dealer wins.

Each **honba** adds **300** bonus points to a winning hand.

> [!NOTE]
> **Honba** is typically tracked using the dealer's **100-point** sticks.

## Renchan

**Renchan** means a dealership repeat.

The dealer keeps their dealership for the next hand if the dealer wins, or the dealer is **tenpai** when the hand ends in a draw.

## Exhaustive draw

Exhaustive draw occurs when there are no more tiles to draw from the **live wall**.

When this happens, players must declare either **tenpai** (and reveal their tenpai hands), or **noten** (and put their hands face down).

The **noten** penalty is **3,000** points, split among all **noten** players and paid to the **tenpai** players.

| Tenpai | Noten | Payment Breakdown |
| :---: | :---: | --- |
| 0 | 4 | No points are exchanged.
| 1 | 3 | The 3 noten players each pay **1,000** points to the tenpai player.
| 2 | 2 | The 2 noten players each pay **1,500** points (each tenpai player receives **1,500** points).
| 3 | 1 | The noten player pays **1,000** points to each of the 3 tenpai players.
| 4 | 0 | No points are exchanged.
