# During a hand

There are many actions a player can take during a hand.
This page explains what happens for every single possible action in detail.

## Chii and Pon

When calling **chii** or **pon**, the steps are as follows:
1. Say "**chii**" or "**pon**" clearly
1. Reveal two tiles from your hand
1. Take the discarded tile to form the meld
1. Place the meld in your bottom-right corner
1. Discard a tile

> [!NOTE]
> Sometimes, players discard a tile immediately after revealing their two tiles before taking the discarded tile.
> This is also acceptable.

For **step 1**, you may not call **chii** or **pon** (or even **kan**) on the last discarded tile.
If there are multiple calls at the same time, **pon** (and **kan**) takes precedence.

For **step 3**, position the discarded tile according to which player you called it from.

```mj
6-57s _ _ _ 77-7z
```

For **step 4**, multiple melds are normally stacked on top of one another.
This is different from online clients where they are placed next to each other.

```mj
2-34p
77-7z
6-57s
```

For **step 5**, be mindful of the [kuikae](../getting-started/kuikae.md) rule!

> [!WARNING]
> The penalty for **kuikae** is a dead hand!

## Kan

When calling **kan**, the steps are similar to **chii** and **pon**:
1. Say "**kan**" clearly
1. Reveal tile(s) from your hand
1. Form the meld and place it in your bottom-right corner
1. The person closest to the **dead wall** reveals a new **dora indicator**
1. Draw a replacement tile from the **dead wall**
1. Discard a tile

> [!NOTE]
> In online clients, the **dora indicator** may be revealed only after you discard a tile.
> The timing for kan dora depends on the ruleset.

### Closed kan

For **closed kan**, reveal all four tiles before turning two face-down.

```mj
2222s _ _ _ 0z22s0z
```

When calling a **closed kan** with "red fives", the "red five" must be face-up.

```mj
0555p _ _ _ 0z50p0z
```

### Open kan

For **open kan**, reveal three tiles, form the meld and place it in your bottom-right corner.

If calling an **open kan** from the player across, both of these melds are acceptable.

```mj
99-99m _ _ _ 999-9m
```

### Added kan

For **added kan**, form the meld by upgrading a **pon** to a **kan** by adding a fourth tile.

```mj
999-s _ _ _ 999=s
```

## Riichi

When declaring **riichi**, the steps are as follows:
1. Say "**riichi**" clearly
1. Discard a tile and place it sideways
1. Place your 1,000-point riichi stick on the compass

For **step 2**, if someone calls on your discarded tile, you must remember to place your next discard sideways.

Also for **step 2**, if someone wins off your discarded tile, **step 3** does not happen. So, you get to keep your riichi stick.

## Tsumo and Ron

When declaring **tsumo**, the steps are as follows:
1. Say "**tsumo**" or "**ron**" clearly
1. Reveal your winning tile (only for **tsumo**)
1. Reveal your hand
1. Reveal the **ura dora indicator(s)** (only if you declared **riichi**)
1. Announce the score of your hand

> [!WARNING]
> You must never take the **ron'd** tile. It is bad etiquette!
