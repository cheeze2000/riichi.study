# Kuikae

**Kuikae** (also known as "swap calling") refers to calling **chii** or **pon** and immediately discarding a tile that could also complete the meld.

**Kuikae** is strictly **forbidden**.

## Restriction on chii

Suppose you call **chii** on `mj 7p` to form the following meld:

```mj
7-56p
```

Discarding `mj 4p` or `mj 7p` is forbidden because both tiles could also complete the meld.

```mj
4-56p _ _ _ 7-56p
```

## Restriction on pon

Suppose you call **pon** on `mj 1z`:

```mj
11-1z
```

Discarding a `mj 1z` is forbidden because it could also complete the meld.

## Another example

Suppose you are going for all simples:

```mj
66m123s67s _ 222-p _ 55-5p
```

You cannot win with `mj 1s` in your hand.

Be careful not to call **chii** on `mj 4s`, because you are not allowed to discard `mj 1s`.
