# Declaring riichi

Declaring **riichi** is one of the most important concepts to learn in riichi mahjong.
Not only does it grant you a **yaku** to declare a valid win, but it can also potentially increase your hand value.

> [!NOTE]
> **Riichi** is the most common yaku in the game according to [statistics](https://tenhou.net/sc/prof.html).

## Open hands vs. closed hands

A hand is **closed** (menzen) if it has not made any **calls**.

Exception: a closed kan does not open the hand.

```mj
0z22s0z
```

## Requirements

To declare **riichi**:
- Your hand must be **closed**
- Your hand must be in **tenpai**

You declare **riichi** by discarding a tile and deposit **1,000** points.

The discarded tile is rotated in the discard pile.

```mj
32z6s153p
4m6z1-s
```

## The riichi autoplay

After declaring **riichi**, your hand is fixed.
Since your hand is in **tenpai**, your winning tile(s) are also fixed.
This means whenever you draw a tile that is not your winning tile, you have to discard it.

At this stage, the game is essentially in autoplay.
You are simply waiting to **tsumo** or **ron**.

## Benefits

Declaring **riichi** has many advantages:
- You earn the deposit back if you win
- Your hand has a guaranteed yaku, **Riichi**
- Your hand may increase in value due to having more dora

> [!NOTE]
> **Dora** is explained in the next section.

## Trade-offs

Declaring **riichi** also has some risks:
- You lose the deposit if you do not win
- Players are more wary and may play defensively
- Due to the autoplay, you might discard unsafe tiles and deal in to other players
