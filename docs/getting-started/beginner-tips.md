# Beginner tips

If you made it this far, you should be ready to play a game now.
You can use these tips to help you in your journey.

## Recommended yaku

Beginners are not expected to memorize every single **yaku** in riichi mahjong.
Instead, it is better to focus on a few simple **yaku** that are easy to remember.

### Riichi - 1 Han

A hand won after declaring riichi.

### All simples (tanyao) - 1 Han

A hand won with only number tiles numbered 2 to 8.

```mj
33m22267s _ 8s _ 4-35p _ 66-6p
```

### Value tiles (yakuhai) - 1 Han

A hand won with a triplet of the value tiles.
Value tiles are **dragons**, **seat winds** and **round winds**.

These triplets are additive, meaning you earn 1 han for each of these triplets in your hand.

```mj
067m1179p345s _ 8p _ 555-z
```

### Seven pairs (chiitoitsu) - 2 Han

A hand won with 7 unique pairs.

```mj
2277m1p330599s44z _ 1p
```

### All triplets (toitoi) - 2 Han

A hand won with four triplets and a pair.
Note that quads behave like triplets.

```mj
444p1188s _ 8s _ 99-9m _ 1111-m
```

### Half flush (honitsu) - 2 Han (open), 3 Han (closed)

A hand won with honor tiles and one suit of number tiles.

```mj
1133366778m _ 5m _ 3-33z
```

### Full flush (chinitsu) - 5 Han (open), 6 Han (closed)

A hand won with only one suit of number tiles.

```mj
1235557s _ 6s _ 4-56s _ 8-79s
```
